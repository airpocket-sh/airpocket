AIRPOCKET · DESIGN SYSTEM · REV 2

Cockpit display language

One multi-function display in a thin dark bezel. Symbology is thin, crisp and colour-coded by avionics convention; the conversation is calm reading text. Every value on screen is real app data. Four signal colours — green, amber, red, sand — on bone, olive and stroke; a dark sector map sits under everything behind frosted glass.

11 · THE MAP LAYER · THE GLASS

--map · ONE SVG · 780×1688 · EIGHT BANDS

A terrain chart seen through glass: eight bands --map-0 (water) to --map-7 (ridge, 1.20× the ground and the brightest the map may be), each 2–3% above the last and filled with a baked-in radial gradient so slopes blend instead of stepping. Contours at 5.5% green, index contours and the coast at 8%, nothing heavier. The terrain lives in the lower third, fades to nothing by the top third and vignettes at the sides; it covers about a third of the chart. One 30px grid at 3%, one range ring at 4.5%, partly off screen. No text, no filters. Ships as one map.svg (flat paths and gradients, ~14 KB once the injected <metadata> block is stripped at build) referenced by URL; each screen shows a different crop via background-position.

--glass · .55BLUR 18 · SAT .85
--glass-2 · .74. A preference for long reading, not a rescue: with the gentler map, --glass is comfortable everywhere.
FALLBACK · --glass-2 · NO BLUR
PANES · FEW AND LARGE

Per screen: the top strip, one content sheet, the bottom panel. Rows are never their own glass. A 1px lighter line along the top edge makes the sheet read as glass. May drop the blur when scrolling costs frames: the session list, the conversation, the terminal, the file viewer, the Controls sheet body. Always blurred: top strip, soft keys, bottom panel, dialogs, first-run and pairing cards. Reduce Transparency (prefers-reduced-transparency): every pane becomes solid --ground with a 1px --stroke edge; the map drops to 40%.

12 · MOTION

WORKING
Radar sweep — green 4s (working), sand 3s (scanning, launch), grey 3s (checking a Mac), amber 6s (retrying) — or an 8px scanning bar (the only glow that moves) on a 2px track, 1.8s each way. Linear, no easing. Only while a turn is running.
NEEDS YOU
Opacity 1 → .4 → 1 over 2.4s, ease-in-out. Stops for good the first time the row is scrolled into view or the session is opened.
SHEETS
Slide up 180ms, out 140ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Toasts fade in 120ms, hold 2.5s, fade 200ms.
IDLE · REDUCED MOTION
Nothing moves at rest, the map included. With prefers-reduced-motion every animation is removed; the sweep becomes a static ring, the bar a static dot, the caution light stays lit.
